How it works

Four steps, in the order a shoot happens. Works for a single artist or a whole roster.

1

Draft

Upload each song once. Tap the tempo, set the downbeat, and mark sections like intro, verse and chorus. They snap to the beat.

2

Plan

Pick the songs and sections you're filming, and build a shot template so every section gets the same coverage.

3

Shoot

Play any section with a click, count-in and lead-in. Slow-mo and speed-up playback. Log and rate every take as you roll.

4

Cut

Every song's timeline with each section and take in one place. Export the take log for your editor.

Working with a band? Send them a private link. They add their photo, songs and sections themselves, and you import it when they're done.
