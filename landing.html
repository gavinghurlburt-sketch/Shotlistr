How it works

Three steps, in the order a shoot happens. Works for a single artist or a whole roster.

Plan

Upload each song once, tap in the tempo and mark its sections. Then pick the songs and sections you're filming, and build a shot template so every section gets the same coverage.

  • Sections snap to the beat
  • Tick a whole song or just the parts you want
  • Shot template, reference images, links and notes

Shoot

On set, tap a section and hit play. The song plays with a click, a count-in and a lead-in, so everyone knows where they are. Log and rate every take as you roll.

  • Slow-mo and speed-up playback
  • Take ratings and locations for your editor
  • Check off each shot as you get it

Cut

Every song's timeline with each section and take in one place. Scrub the song and see exactly what you shot for that moment. Export the take log and send it to your editor.

  • Timeline with sections and takes
  • CSV and PDF take log
  • Works from the first take to the final edit

Working with a band? Send them a private link. They add their photo, songs and sections themselves, and you import it when they're done.
